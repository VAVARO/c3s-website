What do we talk about when we talk about Social Capital?

For us, Social Capital is, in essence, the power that resides in relationships. It is the value, both present and potential, that emerges from our networks of trust and our capacity to cooperate toward a common purpose.

At the same time, it is what unlocks the potential of other forms of capital, such as Human or Physical Capital. It is through the connection between people that the greatest possible value is realized from these resources.

Old Wine in a New Bottle

As a society, we have always valued trust, cooperation, and working together. What is new is that we now have a rigorous concept, widely used across fields, to refer to all of this.

play_arrow

Why Call It "Capital"?

Treating it as capital allows us to understand that it can be built, invested, depleted, or even lost over time. It allows us to measure and compare it—something essential to support better public policies, advance research, and help us become more effective, productive, and fulfilled.

play_arrow

Can Social Capital Be Built?

Absolutely! And our greatest tool is Conversation, as taught by Humberto Maturana. To converse is more than a mere exchange of information; it is the intertwining of "languaging" and "emotioning" where we mutually transform one another.

A community's Social Capital is expressed and reinforced in the quality of its conversations and the conversational agenda it pursues.
