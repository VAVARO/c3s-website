# Translation Notes & Conceptual Mapping (C3S Localization)

This document logs specific Chilean sociotechnological concepts, institutional vocabulary, and conceptual nuances translated from Spanish to English across the C3S website.

---

## 1. Core Terminology & Organizational Phrasing

### 1.1 "Encuentros"
- **Original Spanish Phrase**: *Encuentros* / *Ciclo de Encuentros*
- **Applied English Translation**: *Encounters* / *Cycle of Encounters*
- **Context & Alternatives**: 
  - *Alternatives considered*: Gatherings, Sessions, Forums, Meetups.
  - *Rationale*: In the context of C3S and collaborative initiatives like 3xi (*"El potencial del Encuentro"*), "Encounter" carries a deeper, relational and philosophical weight beyond an informational meeting. It signifies an intentional space for authentic human connection, mutual transformation, and trust-building.

### 1.2 "Círculo Chileno de Capital Social (C3S)"
- **Original Spanish Phrase**: *Círculo Chileno de Capital Social*
- **Applied English Translation**: *Chilean Social Capital Circle (C3S)*
- **Context & Alternatives**: 
  - Standard international naming order preserving the institutional identity and acronym **C3S**.

### 1.3 "Programa de Innovación y Sociotecnología (PISCT)"
- **Original Spanish Phrase**: *Programa de Innovación y Sociotecnología*
- **Applied English Translation**: *Innovation and Sociotechnology Program (PISCT)*
- **Context & Alternatives**: 
  - Academic program at Universidad de Chile (DII). "Sociotechnology" is preserved as it refers specifically to the field developed by Carlos Vignolo and collaborators.

## 2. Roles & Institutional Titles

### 2.1 "Articuladora General" (Dulce Subida)
- **Original Spanish Phrase**: *Articuladora General en Camun; Poeta y Coach Directiva.*
- **Applied English Translation**: *General Articulator at Camun; Poet and Executive Coach.*
- **Context & Alternatives**: 
  - *Alternatives considered*: General Coordinator, Community Weaver, Lead Facilitator.
  - *Rationale*: "Articulador/a" is a recognized title in Latin American social innovation designating the weaving together of diverse ecosystem actors without imposing top-down hierarchy. "General Articulator" preserves this precise nuance.

### 2.2 "Consejera Superior" (Nancy Pérez)
- **Original Spanish Phrase**: *Consejera Superior Universidad Técnica Federico Santa María*
- **Applied English Translation**: *Senior Board Member, Universidad Técnica Federico Santa María*
- **Context & Alternatives**: 
  - Standard English equivalent for highest university governance body member (*Consejo Superior*).

## 3. Ontological & Philosophical Nuances

### 3.1 "Lenguajear y Emocionar" (Maturana Lineage)
- **Original Spanish Phrase**: *"...es el entrecruzamiento de 'lenguajear' y 'emocionar' donde nos transformamos mutuamente."*
- **Applied English Translation**: *"...it is the intertwining of 'languaging' and 'emotioning' where we mutually transform one another."*
- **Context & Alternatives**: 
  - Canonical English academic terminology established in translations of Humberto Maturana and Francisco Varela's works (*Biology of Cognition*, *The Tree of Knowledge*).

### 3.2 "Periplo por el Capital Social"
- **Original Spanish Phrase**: *Periplo por el Capital Social*
- **Applied English Translation**: *Journey for Social Capital*
- **Context & Alternatives**: 
  - *Alternatives considered*: Social Capital Tour, Voyage of Social Capital.
  - *Rationale*: "Journey" captures both the physical itinerary and the exploratory learning mission of Tristan Claridge and the PISCT team across Chile.

### 3.3 "Ganancias Mutuas" (CBI Methodology)
- **Original Spanish Phrase**: *Enfoque de "Ganancias Mutuas"*
- **Applied English Translation**: *"Mutual Gains Approach"*
- **Context & Alternatives**: 
  - Exact trademarked English methodology term used internationally by the Consensus Building Institute (CBI / MIT-Harvard Public Disputes Program).
