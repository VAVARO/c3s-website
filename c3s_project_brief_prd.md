# Project Brief: Círculo Chileno de Capital Social (C3S) Digital Platform

## Project Overview
The Círculo Chileno de Capital Social (C3S) is an organization dedicated to strengthening the social fabric and collaborative innovation in Chile. This project establishes a high-fidelity digital presence that translates their research and activity reports into an interactive, professional, and accessible web experience.

## Vision and Objectives
- **Institutional Identity**: Establish C3S as a leader in social capital research and community building.
- **Knowledge Sharing**: Educate the public on the concept of Social Capital through clear, engaging content.
- **Impact Transparency**: Showcase measurable results from past encounters and workshops using data visualization.
- **Community Engagement**: Provide clear pathways for individuals and organizations to join the network.

## Design System & Branding
- **Primary Palette**: 
  - Burgundy Red (#a20101): Represents passion, social strength, and leadership.
  - Deep Blue (#12488b): Represents trust, depth of research, and institutional stability.
  - Cream Background (#fff6e8): Provides a warm, human, and readable base for content.
- **Typography**: Be Vietnam Pro (Sans-serif) for a modern, professional, and accessible feel.
- **Visual Style**: Clean, academic yet welcoming, utilizing high-quality photography and structured data visualizations.

## Site Structure (Sitemap)
1. **Inicio (Home)**: High-level mission, team introduction, and key impact metrics.
2. **¿Qué es Capital Social? (Education)**: Deep dive into the core concept, including conversational ethics and the "nos salvamos juntos" philosophy.
3. **Encuentros (Case Studies)**: Detailed look at collaborations with organizations like Presencing Institute, Balloon Latam, and CBI.
4. **Resultados (Impact)**: Data-driven page featuring participant mood synthesis, word clouds, and evaluation metrics.
5. **Nuestra Historia (Future)**: Section dedicated to the organization's trajectory and evolution.
6. **Contacto/Unirse (Action)**: Conversion points for newsletter subscription and membership.

## Key Features
- **Data Visualization**: Interactive-style charts for sentiment analysis and participant growth.
- **Word Clouds**: Visual representation of community feedback.
- **Responsive Design**: Optimized for desktop viewing (primary) with mobile-friendly content hierarchy.
- **Resource Downloads**: Integration for CSV datasets and transparency reports.

## Target Audience
- Social innovation leaders and academics.
- NGOs and territorial development organizations.
- Public policy makers and social researchers.
- General public interested in community development in Chile.
